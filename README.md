You are acting as a senior product designer, interaction designer, creative director, narrative designer, and expert frontend engineer.

Build a complete, polished, highly interactive multi-page web experience as a romantic 3-month anniversary gift.

This is NOT a generic romantic website.

This is NOT a landing page.

This is NOT a single long scrolling page.

This is NOT a template.

This should feel like a highly intentional, handcrafted digital artifact made by one person who knows another person extremely well.

The recipient should finish the experience feeling:

"I am actually seen."

"Someone has paid attention to the tiny things about me."

"Someone understands that I am complicated."

"Someone sees both my strengths and the parts of me I am still learning to understand."

"I am loved as a person, not just idealized as a girlfriend."

The experience should be intimate, intelligent, observant, emotionally mature, playful, slightly experimental, aesthetically beautiful, and deeply personal.
